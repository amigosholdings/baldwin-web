FAQ

A few common questions.

How can I track hair loss more accurately?

The main improvement is consistency. Use repeatable photos of the same areas under similar conditions, keep a record of the treatments you are using, and compare progress over time instead of relying on memory or random selfies.

Can Baldwin track hair regrowth as well as hair loss?

Yes. Baldwin is built to track both hair loss and hair regrowth over time using standardized crown and hairline photos, treatment logs, and AI-assisted review.

What can I log in Baldwin?

You can log treatments, consistency, shedding, and side effects alongside your photo history so progress stays connected to what you are actually doing.

Is Baldwin a medical diagnosis tool?

No. Baldwin is a tracking and habit tool designed to help users monitor visual change and treatment consistency over time. It is not a substitute for medical care or diagnosis.
