FAQ

A few common questions.

What Baldwin does, what it does not do, and where it fits.

How can I track hair loss more accurately?

Use repeatable photos of the same areas under similar conditions, keep a record of your treatments, and compare progress over time instead of relying on memory or random selfies.

Can Baldwin track hair regrowth as well as hair loss?

Yes. Baldwin is built to track both hair loss and regrowth using standardized crown and hairline photos, treatment logs, and AI-assisted review.

What can I log in Baldwin?

You can log treatments, consistency, shedding, and side effects alongside your photo history so progress stays connected to what you are actually doing.

Is Baldwin a medical diagnosis tool?

No. Baldwin is a tracking and habit tool. It is not a substitute for medical care or diagnosis.
